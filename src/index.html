Welcome!

Weekdays start as Work from home, so your weeks will initially show as below target (red) until you add office days.

Set your regular in-office days. Start with the Regular in-office days section in Settings. Choose your usual office weekdays to build a base schedule.

Adjust individual days or a date range. At the top you can choose an Edit days tool to click-toggle individual days, or drag from a start day to an ending day to mark a time span of multiple days.

Changes are saved automatically using your browser's Local Storage. You can also export a save file from the Info Dialog, accessible from the info icon in the lower left.

Rolling window overview

Each week: The in-office count is compared with your required days per week in Settings (3 by default). A week qualifies when it meets or exceeds that requirement.

Each 12-week window: A row's Window status covers that week plus the following 11 weeks, not the previous weeks. It shows how many weeks qualify out of 12; hover over it for the window's dates. The Week status shows in-office days against the weekly requirement.

Status: 9–12 qualifying weeks are Passing; exactly 8 are Warning (the minimum is met); fewer than 8 are Failing. The final 11 weeks do not have a complete forward window: their Window status shows a question mark and Not enough data, and their minimap segments are gray. They are excluded from the overview totals, but their individual Week status is still evaluated. Increase the range in Settings to evaluate more windows. If fewer than 12 weeks are configured, all windows have insufficient data.
